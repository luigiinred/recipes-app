Feeds: reader view and viewed posts

Proposal for the Feeds tab. Every post opens as a clean page in the app: recipes as the recipe card, as they do now, and other posts in a new reader view instead of the ad-filled web page. Posts you've read are marked viewed and drop out of the feed. A Show Viewed switch brings them back.

Try it

Things to try:

  • Tap a post to open it. The app checks each post for a recipe. If it finds one, the post opens as the recipe card. If not, it opens in reader view. No switch needed.
  • Go back using ‹ or by dragging the page right. The post is marked viewed and fades out of the list. An Undo toast appears.
  • Drag a card left to mark it viewed without opening it. Drag right to Save it to My Recipes.
  • Open the View menu (≡) and switch Show Viewed on. Viewed posts come back dimmed, with a ✓ badge. Mark All as Viewed is in the same menu.
  • Pick One at a Time in the View menu and swipe up. Each post you swipe past is marked viewed, and the progress bar turns green.
  • In the reader, Aa changes text size, the font (system or New York serif) and the page color (Light, Sepia or Dark).
  • Mark everything viewed to see You're all caught up.
