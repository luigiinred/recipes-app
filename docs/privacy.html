Open Recipes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Open Recipes (the “App”) is a recipe manager for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains how your information is handled.

The short version: the App does not collect, store, or share your personal information. There is no account to create, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Your recipes stay on your devices

Recipes, photos, meal plans, and grocery lists are stored on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your private iCloud Drive. The developer has no access to this data. iCloud sync is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

What the App accesses, and only when you ask

  • Camera — used only to scan a recipe from a book or printout. Scanned images are stored with your recipes.
  • Reminders — used only when you turn on Groceries sync, to keep a list called “Groceries” in step with the App.
  • Network — used only when you import a recipe from a web page or video you provide, so the App can read that page or video.

Embedded video

If you import a recipe from YouTube, the video plays through YouTube’s embedded player, which is provided by Google and may set its own cookies. Google’s use of cookies is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The App is not directed to children and does not collect personal information from anyone.
