Open Recipes — Support

Open Recipes is a recipe manager for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Save recipes from the web or a video, plan your week, and cook step by step.

Getting help

If something isn’t working the way you expect, or you have a feature idea, the fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub:

github.com/luigiinred/recipes-app/issues

Frequently asked questions

Where are my recipes stored?

On your device, and in your private iCloud Drive if you have iCloud enabled. There is no account and no server.

How do I import a recipe?

Tap +, then choose “Import from Web or YouTube…” and paste a link, or scan a page from a book with “Scan Recipe…”.

How do I remove my data?

Delete recipes inside the app, or turn off iCloud for the app to remove the iCloud copy. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
